How to read a record

Every field states its source and its state — Verified, Candidate, Unknown, Not extracted. Nothing is inferred from a sibling record.

What we do not claim

No "verified" badge an institution can buy. No fabricated counts. No invented dates. Candidate websites are leads, not confirmations.

Coverage honesty

An institution with no published leadership page shows none — not an assumed value. Coverage varies by source; the ingestion page shows exactly which.
